The Artist Behind The Map

Sharon Jemima

Sharon is a British artist who moved to Porto and never looked back. She's spent years wandering the city's tiled staircases, riverside terraces and half-forgotten backstreets — always with a sketchbook.

Her work grew out of a simple frustration: standard city maps don't tell you anything. They show you where to go, but not why. Sharon's illustrated walking route scripts fix that — they're part map, part journal, part love letter to a city that rewards curiosity.

The guided tours came next. Sharon began taking small groups through the same routes she'd already drawn, and discovered that Porto looks completely different when someone who actually lives there is pointing things out. Tours are kept to a maximum of eight people — small enough that nobody gets lost, large enough to spark good conversation.
